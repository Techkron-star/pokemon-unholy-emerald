# Unholy — The Black Economy

Status: design baseline approved in conversation; Phase 1 catalog/pricing implementation in this branch, not yet compiled. Numerical balance below is proposed unless explicitly stated. Do not treat future phases as implemented.

## Goals and boundaries
Calagua sells certainty; Horizon sells opportunities. Bounties pay mixed rewards, raids provide materials/loot/captures, wild bosses provide artifacts/captures, and the Cofradía is the extreme money-only farming activity. No story bypass, permanent Pokémon confiscation, or replacement of exploration-based artifacts.

## P1 — Calagua
1F reception, directory and lottery; 2F capture and medicine; 3F EV training, all 21 nature mints, five Exp. Candy sizes (not Rare Candy), abilities and IV resources; 4F TMs and competitive held items; 5F Mega Stones, all elemental and species Z-Crystals, evolutionary items and optional transformation resources. Preserve decoration catalogs and rooftop story weather guards. In this initial implementation the four 5F clerks ask whether to open the new catalog; NO opens their original decoration catalog. Special form items currently use the fourth counter rather than relocating NPCs to the roof. Prices are scoped to the department store, must exceed resale value, and must not alter global item prices. Existing item IDs and handlers reused. Mega/Z availability must be verified against enabled species/form configurations.
Proposed baseline: regular Mega Stones 50k, legendary 120k, special variants 80k; Z type 25k, signature 60k–100k; Exp. Candy 300/1000/3000/9000/24000; mints 15k. Keep Master Balls out of the permanent shop. Device unlock items remain crafting-only. The stock can be permanent once the city is accessible; further story gates can be applied to the few exceptional form items if needed.

## P2 — Horizon V2
Current Pokémon offers by watch tier are 3/4/6; proposed 3/6/12. Goods 5/8/12, TMs 3/6/12, eggs unchanged. Keep separate categories and day/night rotation. Maximum watch gets more offers and improved deal probabilities. Preserve standard market mutation limits; do not silently expand them.
Horizon prices 30–60% below Calagua reference, rare discounts up to 90%. Promotion means N units for one listed price, normally 2–10x. For key items, one unit, discount only. Regular promotions are substantially more common on basic consumables than on legendary/mythical goods. Proposed mutually exclusive per-slot chances (promotion / chromatic): common 65%/3%; uncommon 50%/1.5%; rare 35%/0.8%; epic 20%/0.3%; legendary 10%/0.1%; mythical 5%/0.05%. Remaining is normal. Item rarity roll independent of promotion; adjust rates in tests. Chromatic deals: discount 85–99% against the price of one unit, enormous quantity (up to 99 for common, 30 for rare, 10 for Master Ball; key items 1). Master Ball can have 10x1 and chromatic deals. Do not expose global price=0 as a free transaction. Show quantity, full payable price, rare badge, rotation, and sold state. Commit once per purchase; no repeat on reopening, bag-full or money failure must leave stock intact. Prevent buy/resell arbitrage via provenance-aware resale limits or an explicit safe policy; never silently nerf legitimate normal resale globally.
Whitelisted optional keys (DNA Splicers, N-Solarizer, N-Lunarizer, Reins of Unity, Prison Bottle, Rotom Catalog, Zygarde Cube, Gracidea, Reveal Glass) can rotate at reduced prices; already-owned unique items removed. No plot keys, HMs, watch or access bypass. Crafting-exclusive devices excluded from commerce.
Storage: existing Pokémon status word uses sold bits 0–5 and seen bit 6; move to a versioned dedicated 12-bit sold mask and independent seen state, preserve old six offers/sold flags across migration. Do not alias existing save variables. Persist or deterministically reconstruct goods, item price, quantity, tier and offer grade per rotation; separate sold masks. Use validated save schema and tests for rollback, old saves, invalid clocks, upgrade mid-rotation and inventory full.

## P3 — Craftable transformation devices
Mega Ring, Z-Power Ring, Dynamax Band, Tera Orb: one-time permanent craftable key items; proposed unlock badges 4/5/6/7, subject to pacing review. Recipes: 3 Mega fragments + 2 rare minerals + catalyst; 3 Z fragments + 2 elemental gems + Z core; 5 Dynamax particles + 2 Wishing fragments; 5 Tera shards + 2 pure crystals + Tera core. Audit existing crafting material IDs and unlocks first, avoid duplicate IDs, check all battle flags and device checks. Max Soup uses 3 Max Mushrooms (+ Max Honey for Urshifu) to toggle G-Max factor on compatible species, preserve chromatic identity, mutations, artifacts and save. Existing Max Mushrooms are battle stat items: soup is a distinct craft/service, do not change existing effect silently. Tera shards for tera-type changes, Dynamax candies for level. Preserve mechanic restrictions and ensure valid encounter/battle support.

## P4 — Economy
Audit wallet storage, UI widths, money API, multiplication and overflow, purchasing and sales before raising proposed cap to 999,999,999. Keep higher intermediate values in checked u64 and avoid truncation. Preserve existing saves. Add transactional tests and price catalog shared by Horizon; avoid zero-cost and arbitrage. Do not use real currency; all stakes are in-game money.

## P5 — Cofradía / Trainer Hill
Repurpose Trainer Hill (Route 111 north of Mauville); preserve maps/story access until explicit unlock review. Money only, no EXP/EV or loot, team restored between battles and personal held/consumed items not permanently lost. Custom stake >= minimum, funded by cash or optional credit. Proposed difficulties minimum/multiplier per victory: Normal 5k/1.01, Hard 15k/1.03, Expert 35k/1.07, Master 75k/1.12, Infernal 150k/1.20, Abyss 300k/1.35. Rounding deterministic. Higher difficulty scales enemy AI, team synergy, artifacts, mutations, battle rules and boss quality, not just cash. Escrow = stake; each win compounds total; cash out after any win, no adding funds mid-run. Defeat forfeits entire escrow including unclaimed profit; no duplicate credit deduction. Track records and persist run state before combat; define reset/abandon resolution and recovery UI. Boss every five wins proposed; endless until cap or retreat; use safe arithmetic and disclose cap before play.

## P6 — Debt and reversible mixed collateral
Optional loan with clear consequences, no new loan while in debt; no interest or deadline. On loss, outstanding borrowed principal becomes debt; never double-charge cash already escrowed. Prioritize highest-value Pokémon across party/PC by actual stats and mutation rarity/count; species rarity and level secondary (suggested weights 40/35/15/10; normalize before weighting). If inadequate, combine Pokémon with artifacts, rare and regular inventory items, and optional non-story key items. Do not confiscate required story keys, HMs, HF Watch, or enough Pokémon to prevent progression. All collateral is temporary: no sale, deletion, stat/identity changes, irreversible transfer or expiry. Payment in installments allowed; full payoff returns all held assets. Voluntary permanent item payment is a separate option only with explicit confirmation; no automatic sale. Bóveda holds complete checksummed Pokémon data, including PID/OT, chromatic version, mutations, held items and artifacts; respect fusion ownership and attached inventories. Prevent duplicates with atomic write/rollback, capacity checks and a recoverable pending-return state if party/PC/bag full. Validate custody save size and old save migration before any live confiscation. Ensure accessible repayment sources and a minimum viable team. Explicit in-game warning of stake, loan amount and potential seizure, with debt/asset view.

## P4 implementation note (wallet branch)
- Cash expands to the full existing encrypted u32 range (4,294,967,295). Unsigned conversion used for core money, trainer card, sales, purchase quotes and battle reward/loss text; formatter arithmetic no longer uses signed multiplication. No SaveBlock1 field movement. Saturating cash additions remain in place.
- SELECT on the local trainer card opens wallets: cash, reserve, casino coins, BP. The reserve holds up to 999,999,999,999, using an independently bounded CRC-covered record at Save4 offset 1024. Deposit/withdraw amounts selected via D-pad and L/R steps; reserve not spendable directly, no interest, no currency conversions. No wallet access from remote trainer cards. Legacy saves initialize the reserve to zero. Unknown or invalid reserve data refuses transactions. Transfers preserve total balance within RAM and normal save flow; arbitrary flash corruption/save-state rollback cannot be guaranteed safe.
- Rydel's original free bicycle offer and swaps remain. Declining the travel question offers either bike for 1,000,000; confirm, choose, verify room and funds, give bike, then charge and set received flag. Free gift now sets received only after successful grant. This paid route is optional and deliberately expensive; no forced purchase.
- Tests added for unsigned cash, format and reserve transfer. Compilation, emulator layout, script localization, whole-repository audit of signed money consumers, and earlier PRs' QA remain pending. No debt or tower logic in this phase.

## P5C — Wager engine and playable lobby loop
Six cash-only difficulties and exact integer percentage compounding (101/103/107/112/120/135 over 100), arbitrary stake from minimum through full cash, separate escrow, one result per armed battle, cash-out after a win, loss/forfeit clears entire escrow, and personal records. Provisional badge gates 3/4/5/6/7/8. A dedicated versioned CRC-covered Save4 record starts at 1088; wallet bounds tightened. Every round requires normal in-game save with FIGHTING state before entering battle; reopening a persisted interrupted round forfeits it. Cancelling the initial save refunds the stake in RAM; cancelling later round restores ready state. This does not prevent external save-state rollback. Save after a win/cash-out to preserve it; automatic post-battle flash save not yet implemented. Funds cap at MAX_MONEY, cash-out refuses insufficient wallet room; player can deposit cash in reserve. 
Initial opponents reuse existing bounty trainers and Elite Four/Champion/Steven data, deterministic selection and every fifth battle as boss. Normal trainer battle callback specifically bypasses whiteout; full party restored and healed, no EXP/EV through EXP gate, no normal reward or Pay Day, no bag use, no evolution. Existing default battle message may show zero money. Script is playable in lobby rather than dedicated new floor maps. Custom enemy teams, mutation/gear ramp, boss art, debt, loans, and custody are deliberately not enabled in this patch. New dialogue Spanish, dynamic menu bilingual. Compile and emulator QA pending.

P5C follow-up: opponents scale upward to player highest level with difficulty offsets (-5/-2/0/+2/+4/+7), streak increase capped +15 and boss +2, without lowering original trainer levels. Higher difficulties enable stronger AI and staged enemy mutations (Alpha, Hyper, Ancestral, late Abyss boss Omega). Custom gear loadouts and bespoke enemy teams still pending. These are provisional balance values.
